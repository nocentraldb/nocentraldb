# 2026-11-08 · NOC-44 · GENESIS.md follows RFC 0008's M10 on groups

- Decided by: Carsten
- Why: GENESIS.md said groups can be members of many spaces, which RFC 0008 defers (M10, conflict 1). The owner decided that GENESIS.md says what v1 delivers: a member is a person's device, an agent or a sub-agent; for now a list of members that the SDK expands into single members stands in for a group; groups that are members of spaces in their own right come later. NOC-44 rewrites the "Spaces" bullet and the trade-off table, and RFC 0001 and the FAQ use the same definition.
- Instead of: leaving GENESIS.md's groups sentence in place until a later ticket.
