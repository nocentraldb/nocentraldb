- Why: every agent read the whole decision log at start (about 137 KB, some 35,000 tokens) although `pnpm decisions --index` gives one line per decision in 17 KB; agents now read the index and in full only the decisions of their ticket, the tickets it depends on and the topics it touches. A departure from an RFC, or an open choice developers can see, is never decided in a PR: it becomes a needs-owner ticket.
