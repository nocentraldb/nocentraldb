# 2026-11-19 · NOC-360 · The owner's landing-page texts and the mark in the header

- Decided by: Carsten, 2026-11-19, in chat with pm (batch 1 of his review of #383, relayed by pm with pm's grammar and glossary fixes, each of which the owner can overrule)
- Why: the landing page leads with what a visitor gets, in words a visitor knows. The hero reads "Reads like local files, syncs like shared docs", and its lead (also the page's description) says the database puts the data next to the people and agents who use it while the server only ever sees encrypted data. The scenario is "Two people, one of them travelling, and an agent work on the same task list", in four steps told from the traveller: "Everyone has the full task list", "A change travels encrypted", "The traveller goes offline", "The traveller reconnects". The header shows the yellow `ncdb` mark beside the wordmark, and only the mark on a phone; the link keeps the name NoCentralDB. pm's fixes: "everyone" for "members" in the lead, since a visitor does not know the term; "person" and "traveller" for "user", which the glossary avoids; "reading needs no network", since syncing does; "carry on from the last state they know" for "don't care" and "are not aware"; British spelling, as the site's prose uses it.
- Instead of: the headline "A database that reads like a local file and syncs like a shared doc", the lead "Each person and each agent reads and writes a full copy on their own machine…", the scenario "One task list on a train, in a home office and with an agent" with its four steps of 2026-11-12, and a header with the wordmark alone.
