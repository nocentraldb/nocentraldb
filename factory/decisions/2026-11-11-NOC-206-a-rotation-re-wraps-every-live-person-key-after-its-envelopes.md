# 2026-11-11 · NOC-206 · A rotation re-wraps every live person key after its envelopes, and refuses one it does not hold

- Decided by: coding agent (NOC-206, part 3)
- Why: RFC 0003 ("Removing a member and rotating the space key", step 3) and RFC 0008 ("Key chain entry") have a rotation re-wrap every person-key record under the new generation, but leave the order of the changes and what a member does without a key to the SDK. The SDK's rotation writes, in its one batch, the key chain entry, the envelopes, then one person-key entry per live person key (one an accepted entry created and no shred destroyed). Each entry depends on the envelopes, names the fresh generation in its header and as its record's generation, and repeats the key's commitment, as RFC 0008 requires. Its record is wrapped under the fresh key with NOC-209's wrap, signed by the rotating admin, and collected by the batch. Once the batch is folded, the caller hands the records to syncing, which puts them after the push, so the relay replaces the old wrap. What the call writes after the rotation (a grant, an add) depends on the envelopes and the person-key entries. A rotation in a space with a live person key the admin does not hold is refused before anything is written.
- Instead of: skipping a person key the admin does not hold, which would leave its old wrap at the relay under a generation a removed member knows, against step 3; re-wrapping a shredded key, which the relay would refuse and which must stay destroyed; writing the person-key entries before the envelopes, which would announce records under a generation no member yet holds a key to.
- What this does not do: a member that holds no copy of a person key yet (its fetch has not run) cannot rotate until it has fetched the record. A record that fails is still ignored without naming its writer; `SpaceStatus::bad_person_key_records` follows in its own PR (NOC-222 point 2).
