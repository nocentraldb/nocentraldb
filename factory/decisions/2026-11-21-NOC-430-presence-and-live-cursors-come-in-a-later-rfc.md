# 2026-11-21 · NOC-430 · Presence and live cursors in rich text come in a later RFC, with an encrypted channel for messages that are not changes

- Decided by: Carsten, 2026-11-21, on RFC 0013's D8, through pm
- Why: cursors need messages that are not signed into the log, not kept, encrypted for the space and relayed at once, which is a relay feature of its own with metadata of its own.
- Instead of: designing the relay's ephemeral channel in RFC 0013.
- Topics: rich-text, rfc-0013, relay, presence
