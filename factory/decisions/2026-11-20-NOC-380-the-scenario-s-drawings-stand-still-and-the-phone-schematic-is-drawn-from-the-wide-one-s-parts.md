# 2026-11-20 · NOC-380 · The scenario's drawings stand still, and the phone schematic is drawn from the wide drawing's parts

- Decided by: coding agent, NOC-380 (for pm's review)
- Why: with nothing scroll-driven the scenario's per-step animation has no job left. Each step's wide drawing is drawn still by `scene(step, id, "wide", { still: true })`: the picture the moving drawing shows 0.72 s into its loop, which is the picture the middle width held before, so a tablet sees what it saw. A frozen point is an `animateMotion` with one key point and `fill="freeze"`, so the drawing needs no path geometry in JavaScript and no script at all. The scenario's pause control goes with the motion; the hero keeps its moving drawing and its pause control, since the hero is not scroll-driven (it shows from 640 px only). The phone schematic the owner asked for (`scene(step, id, "phone")`, viewBox 400x260) reuses the wide drawing's parts: the train with its hill and tunnel, the house and the server rack at 0.38 of their size, the same cloud, links and colours; each copy is a card of three bars, one per task, marked yellow where a change starts and outlined where one arrived, as the wide drawing marks its rows. A change's way is a yellow arrow, and only step 2 shows the lock, since it is the step about encryption. It holds no text and no motion, and is drawn at most 400 px wide. The kit's `moments/` files, `scene-wheel.mjs`, the snap rules, the moment preloads and the flick e2e test existed only for the pinned scenario and are removed, and so is the tall phone drawing the schematic replaces. `site/scripts/scenario-stacked.test.mjs` and `site/scripts/scene.test.mjs` pin it in the gate; `scripts/site-demo/scenario-stacked.e2e.test.mjs` checks it in Chromium.
- Instead of: keeping the animation and pausing it by script, as the middle width did, which keeps a script and a control for a drawing that should not move; a lock on the cloud in every step, which would blur step 2's one cue; new icons for the phone, which would make the phone's story look unlike the wide one's.
