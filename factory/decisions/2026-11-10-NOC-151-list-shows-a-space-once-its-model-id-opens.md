# 2026-11-10 · NOC-151 · `spaces().list()` shows a space once its model id opens

- Decided by: coding agent (NOC-151), the safe subset
- Why: RFC 0009 says `db.spaces.list()` shows every space of the member, a space of another model with that state. Whether a space is of another model is known only once the copy holds its genesis and the key of its first generation, which opens the sealed `model.id` (NOC-119). So `list()` returns a space from then on, with `SpaceModel::Ours` or `SpaceModel::Another`, and leaves it out before, as NOC-119's decision said. It does not ask for the key of the current generation, as `spaces().get` does: a removed member keeps its copy, and its space stays listed with `role: None`, also after a rotation it holds no key of. `role` is the member's role in the folded membership, `None` when the member was removed or no entry of it has been folded. The list comes in the order of the space ids.
- Instead of: listing a space whose model id cannot be opened yet as `Another`, which would tell an app its user cannot open a space that is of its own model, or leaving out every space `get` refuses with `NotFound`, which would hide a removed member's space.
- What this does not do: a member that joined after a rotation and holds only later keys does not see the space in the list until key chain entries give it the first generation's key, just as `get` answers `NotFound` for it. RFC 0009 writes `[{ id, role, model, ... }]` but names no further field, so `SpaceInfo` has these three, as the owner decided on NOC-135. A removed member's copy learns of its removal only where it folds the removal; the relay serves no change to a removed member, so `role` stays the last one folded until another path brings the removal.
