# 2026-11-20 · NOC-380 · The landing page's story is stacked steps at every width, without scroll behaviour

- Decided by: Carsten, 2026-11-20, in chat with pm (NOC-380)
- Why: the scroll behaviour of the scenario was a nice idea, but it did more harm than good. The story "Two people, one of them travelling, and an agent work on the same task list" is told in its steps one under the other at every width, as the middle width (640–1023 px) showed it: each step's title, text and drawing. Nothing pins, snaps, takes over the wheel or a gesture, or moves with the scroll. From 640 px a step is a row, its words at the left and its drawing at the right; a phone stacks the drawing under the words. On a phone (below 640 px) no text in a drawing is larger than the step's body text. The demo, "Get started" and the close are unchanged. This replaces the pinned scenario of 2026-11-12-no-ticket-the-landing-page-uses-near-black-and-yellow-with-magenta-only.md ("one scroll scenario of four steps") and, in 2026-11-19-NOC-360-the-landing-page-leads-with-reads-like-local-files-syncs.md, the screen of scroll per step with a snap point (option b) and the gesture lock. Both files stay current for everything else they decide (the colours, the texts, the four steps, the header, the code section, the demo), so neither is marked superseded as a whole.
- Instead of: the kit's pinned-chapters moment from 1024 px, with one screen of scroll per step, scroll snapping (option b) and a wheel and gesture lock that moved it by one step per gesture.
