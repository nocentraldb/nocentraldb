# 2026-11-10 · NOC-147 · The log keeps each author's relay counts in memory, and the sync status sums them

- Decided by: coding agent (NOC-147)
- Why: `SyncReport::current()` decoded every change of every space's log to count this member's unconfirmed and refused changes (NOC-90). The ticket put counters on the SDK's `Inner`, updated in `space.rs`, on confirmation and on refusal. But every change reaches the log through `View::receive`: the SDK's writes, `members().add`'s control changes, and changes sync pulls, its own among them after a reopen. Every confirmation, refusal and new relay epoch goes through `View::confirm`, `View::refuse` and `View::forget_relay_positions`. So the core's `Log` keeps a `RelayCounts { unconfirmed, refused }` per author, as it keeps its tips (NOC-138): an appended change counts as unconfirmed, a first confirmation or refusal moves it, a refused change stays refused even when the relay also confirmed it, and a new epoch returns every confirmed, unrefused change to unconfirmed. Each entry keeps whether the relay confirmed it, in memory beside the refusal it already kept. The view reads that back from `relay_position` when it loads the log, after a reopen, a refold or a rolled-back receive. `View::relay_counts(space, author)` reads them, and `current()` sums them over the database's spaces for its own member key. The view's tables and format version do not change.
- Instead of: counters on `Inner`, which would need a hook at every write site (`space.rs`, `members/`) and in sync, and a rebuild that decodes the log on open; or a column for the author in the `log` table, a change of the on-disk format, which is the owner's.
- What this does not do: the counts cover only what a full recount of `View::unconfirmed` and `View::refused` covers. Opening still decodes every frame once, as loading the log already did.
