# 2026-11-11 · NOC-211 · The landing page is 60/30/10: near-black, safety yellow and hot magenta

- Decided by: Carsten, 2026-11-11, on NOC-211
- Why: about 60 % near-black (the brand's `bg` #111110 and its surfaces), about 30 % the brand's safety yellow #F2DC4B as grounds and panels, and about 10 % a loud third colour, hot magenta #FF3D8B, for the primary action, the connected state, link underlines and focus. The owner chose magenta from three candidates (signal blue, safety green, hot magenta) as "a good starting point". On nocentraldb.io this overrides BRAND.md's rule that the accent covers under 5 % of a screen; the other rules of the NoCentralDB brand (nxsflow/brand, commit 38abcfc) stand. The site keeps the colour as one token, `--loud`, next to the brand's `--ncdb-*` roles; the docs take the same three roles.
- Instead of: BRAND.md's rule as written, yellow under 5 % on near-black with no third colour; or signal blue or safety green as the loud colour.
