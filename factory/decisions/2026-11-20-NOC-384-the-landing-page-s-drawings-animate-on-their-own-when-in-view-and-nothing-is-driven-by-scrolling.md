# 2026-11-20 · NOC-384 · The landing page's drawings animate on their own when in view; nothing on the page is driven by scrolling

- Decided by: Carsten, 2026-11-20, to pm (NOC-384)
- Why: a drawing on the landing page plays its animation by itself, in place, once it comes into view. Scrolling only decides whether it is visible, never how far it has played, and nothing pins, snaps or takes over the wheel or a gesture. Under reduced motion every drawing shows a still picture, and the page offers a way to pause the motion. On a phone (below 640 px) the scenario's steps show their text-free schematics. This replaces NOC-380's still scenario drawings (that entry is now a comment on NOC-380, which still holds for how the phone schematic is drawn and for the removed scroll code).
- Instead of: the still scenario drawings of NOC-380, and the pinned, scroll-driven scenario before them.
