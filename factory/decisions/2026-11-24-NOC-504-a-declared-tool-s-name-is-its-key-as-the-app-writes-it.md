# 2026-11-24 · NOC-504 · A declared tool's name is its key in nc.tools, as the app writes it

- Decided by: Carsten, 2026-11-24, on RFC 0017's open questions, through pm (NOC-504)
- Why: the model and the logs show what the developer typed, no two keys can turn into one name, and MCP prescribes no case.
- Instead of: converting names to snake case, which shows names nobody typed and can merge two keys into one.
- Topics: mcp, public-api, rfc-0017
