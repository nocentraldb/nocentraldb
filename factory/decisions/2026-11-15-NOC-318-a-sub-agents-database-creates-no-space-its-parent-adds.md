# 2026-11-15 · NOC-318 · A sub-agent's database creates no space; its parent adds it to the spaces it works in

- Decided by: pm, NOC-318, on the coding agent's proposal
- Why: a database's own certificate is read only when it creates a space, whose genesis carries the creator's certificate; a sub-agent's writes are checked against the add entry in its space, and its invitation is always version 3. So a sub-agent that creates no space needs no certificate of its own on disk, and no new form of the key record. `Spaces::create` on a sub-agent's database fails with `NotAllowed` (`error.subAgentCreatesNoSpace`): its parent adds it to the spaces it works in, as RFC 0003 has it work in the ad-hoc space its parent made. `Members::add` of a sub-agent's invitation runs only on an agent's database, which signs the certificate with its member key under its account key and statement; on a device's or a sub-agent's database it fails with `NotAllowed` (`error.onlyAgentAddsSubAgent`). A sub-agent adding a sub-agent of its own, which RFC 0003 allows, needs its parent's account key and statement, which it could read from its own add entry; it is a follow-up ticket. Whether a sub-agent may ever create a space, which would need a key-record form for its certificate, is a later question for the owner (NOC-334).
- Instead of: a new form of the key record now, for a certificate no call of this slice reads; or a sub-agent's certificate kept in memory and learnt again on every open, which would leave a sub-agent unable to act offline after a reopen.
- Topics: sub-agents, accounts, sdk-rust
