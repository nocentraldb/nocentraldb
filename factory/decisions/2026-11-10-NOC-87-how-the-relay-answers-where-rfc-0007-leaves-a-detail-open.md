# 2026-11-10 · NOC-87 · How the relay answers where RFC 0007 leaves a detail open

- Decided by: coding agent (NOC-87)
- Why: each frame of a push gets its own result, so every refusal of a frame is a `rejected` result, `not_member`, `unknown_space` and `space_exists` included: the frames are independent, and a membership entry in the same push can change who may upload the next one. A frame whose header names another space than the push is `malformed` for that push. The uploader may be a current member of any role, as RFC 0007 says. A space the relay does not hold accepts only its genesis, uploaded by its creator. What the relay cannot check, because it does not know it, waits for an upgraded relay as `unsupported_suite`: an unknown suite, kind, header format or "must understand" header key, and a control body `resolve` parks. `resolve`'s rejections map to `bad_signature` (bad signature), `author_not_member` (an author no held entry names), `malformed` (a body that is not a control body) and `membership_rule` (every other rejection). The checks run cheapest first: size, decoding, space, suite, then against the space at its head the uploader, a duplicate, the author and the rules.
- Instead of: refusing a whole push for one frame; or `membership_rule` for a body the relay does not understand, which tells the member to re-check a change that no member's control change will ever make the relay accept.
