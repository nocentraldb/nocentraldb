# 2026-11-10 · NOC-106 · RFC 0003 and RFC 0009 take NOC-106's dated line in their changelog, and RFC 0007 gets a History section

- Decided by: coding agent (NOC-106)
- Why: the RFC README asks for a dated History line, but RFC 0003 and RFC 0009 are not yet rewritten (NOC-100, NOC-101) and keep their dated lines in the "Changelog" at the top; a second dated list beside it would split their history. RFC 0007 had no dated list, so it gets the "History" section the rewritten RFCs use, with the lines git shows for it. The rewrites move the changelog lines into History.
- Instead of: a History section in RFC 0003 and RFC 0009 next to their changelog, or renaming their changelog, which is the rewrite's work.
