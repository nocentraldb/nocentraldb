# 2026-11-11 · NOC-169 · `members().set_role` rotates only when it gives a reader the right to write, and the last admin may not lower itself

- Decided by: coding agent (NOC-169)
- Why: RFC 0008 calls a set role that gives the right to write to a member that lost it, or to a reader, a re-grant, which needs a fresh generation with no envelope to the subject in the grant's causal past ("Membership entry", "A member demoted and raised again"). So a raise from reader to writer or admin rotates first, leaving the subject out of the rotation's envelopes, writes the set role from the fresh generation, and writes the subject's envelope after it, depending on the grant. Every other set role writes no rotation: lowering a role keeps the right to read, which no rotation could take back, and raising a writer to admin keeps a right to write the subject already holds; its from generation is the current one. Any member may lower its own role (RFC 0008, "Roles and rights"); raising one, or setting another member's, needs an admin. The space's last admin lowering its own role is refused with RFC 0009's `lastAdmin` (`ErrorCode::LastAdmin`, added), as `space.leave()` is for the last admin. A set role to the role the member holds is written like any other.
- Instead of: rotating on every demotion, which would cost one envelope per member and keep nothing from a reader; writing the subject's envelope inside the rotation, which puts it in the grant's causal past so the fold rejects the re-grant; refusing a set role to the same role, which RFC 0008 does not forbid.
- What this does not do: it does not stop two admins who lower each other or the last two admins who lower themselves at the same time; RFC 0008 lets a space lose its last admin in the concurrent case.
