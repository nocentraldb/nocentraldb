# 2026-11-20 · NOC-380 · The scenario's drawings stand still, and a phone draws them at most 400 px wide with type of 16 px at most

- Decided by: coding agent, NOC-380 (for pm's review)
- Why: with nothing scroll-driven the scenario's per-step animation has no job left. Each step's drawing is drawn still by `scene(step, id, layout, { still: true })`: the picture the moving drawing shows 0.72 s into its loop, which is the picture the middle width held before, so the tablet layout looks as it did. A frozen point is an `animateMotion` with one key point and `fill="freeze"`, so the drawing needs no path geometry in JavaScript and no script at all. The scenario's pause control goes with the motion; the hero keeps its moving drawing and its pause control, since the hero is not scroll-driven (it shows from 640 px only). On a phone the tall drawing (viewBox 400 wide) is at most 400 px wide and sets its type at 16 px at most (label and title 16, status 15, cipher 14, the lens 16), so its text is drawn at no more than the step's 16 px body text at any phone width; at 375 px the largest is about 13 px. The kit's `moments/` files, `scene-wheel.mjs`, the snap rules, the moment preloads and the flick e2e test existed only for the pinned scenario and are removed. `site/scripts/scenario-stacked.test.mjs` pins it in the gate; `scripts/site-demo/scenario-stacked.e2e.test.mjs` measures it in Chromium.
- Instead of: keeping the animation and pausing it by script, as the middle width did, which keeps a script and a control for a drawing that should not move; a narrower third layout of the drawing for phones; or shrinking the tall drawing's width to fit its old, larger type.
