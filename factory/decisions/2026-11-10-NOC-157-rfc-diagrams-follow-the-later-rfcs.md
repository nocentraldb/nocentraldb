# 2026-11-10 · NOC-157 · The RFC diagrams draw what the later, more specific RFCs decided, where RFC 0001's prose is ahead of them

- Decided by: pm (NOC-157)
- Why: RFC 0001 says rich text and streams ship, that adapters feed EventBridge and that apps read views, while RFC 0004, 0005, 0007 and 0009 reserve rich text, streams, views and computed fields and make an adapter a member of the space; a diagram that draws RFC 0001's wording would show what is not designed. The diagrams therefore draw only declared indexes, basic values, sets and links, and an adapter as a member, and RFC 0001's prose is aligned in NOC-161.
- Instead of: drawing RFC 0001's wording as it stands, or changing RFC 0001's prose in the diagrams' pull request.
- Topics: rfc-0001, docs, diagrams
