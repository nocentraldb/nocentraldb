# 2026-11-15 · NOC-325 · A device signs off a space it does not hold only for an agent of its own account

- Decided by: pm, NOC-325, on the coding agent's proposal, carrying out the owner's NOC-326 Q4
- Why: the owner's Q4 has a device of the account sign off an erasure in a space whose members are only agents of that account, such as an ad-hoc copy; the device is no member there, so it holds no copy to check the sign-off against. The erasure request therefore carries the performer's certificate, which is public, as an invitation carries it. `Account::sign_off` signs a space it does not hold only when that certificate names the performer, is an agent's, and is signed with this device's own account key. Such a sign-off holds only where every member belongs to the device's account, and makes the erasure provisional; the performer's `Db::erase` checks every sign-off against the space before it writes anything. A sub-agent's certificate is signed by its parent, not the account, so it does not qualify yet; in an ad-hoc copy the performer is the parent agent, a member of both spaces.
- Instead of: a device that signs any space it does not hold without a check, whose account could then also sign off a space of other people where another of its devices is an admin, unseen; and requiring the device to be a member of the space, which contradicts a space of agents only.
- Topics: sdk-rust, erasure, agents
