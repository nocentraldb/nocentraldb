# 2026-11-19 · NOC-362 · RFC 0001 opens with what the product is for, as the landing page's hero does

- Decided by: Carsten, 2026-11-19, in chat with pm, on NOC-362 (#387)
- Why: the RFC's first lines should say what NoCentralDB is for, in the words the landing page leads with: "Reads like local files, syncs like shared docs", with the data next to the people and agents who use it and a relay that only ever sees encrypted data. "The problem" ends on the same point: the apps where the data belongs with the people and agents who use it.
- Instead of: the old opening, "For when a central database is the wrong place", which said what the product is not for.
