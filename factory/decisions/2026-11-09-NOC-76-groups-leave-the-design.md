# 2026-11-09 · NOC-76 · Groups leave the design for now

- Decided by: Carsten (owner), 2026-11-09, in chat with pm
- Why: groups caused more problems than they solved. No definition names them for now: a member is a person's device, an agent or a sub-agent, and nothing else. RFC 0008's M10 and RFC 0009's S14 are marked withdrawn by the owner, not deleted. RFC 0001 Amendment 5, RFC 0008 Amendment 1 and RFC 0009 Amendment 1 carry it out, and the FAQ follows. What remains is `members.add` taking a list of invitations, as plain batching. If a later need shows groups are useful, they come back through a new RFC. The owner already removed the groups sentence from GENESIS.md's "Spaces" bullet (#76, 33da4ae).
- Instead of: keeping RFC 0008's M10, a list of members the SDK expands into each space, until a later RFC designs groups as members.
