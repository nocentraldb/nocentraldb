# 2026-11-11 · NOC-220 · An admin's SDK writes the merge rotation on its own, after a random delay of up to a minute

- Decided by: coding agent (NOC-220), on the plan pm approved
- Why: RFC 0008, "Two removals that rotate the key at once", step 5, has the SDK of any admin that sees an exposed current generation write a merge rotation after a random delay of up to a minute, unless it sees another admin's first; a member that leaves cannot rotate for itself. After each pass of the sync session over the spaces, every space where this member is an admin, not stopped, with an exposed current generation, gets a deadline at a random delay from the platform's secure generator, at most a minute; a space no longer exposed loses its deadline, so another admin's merge rotation, folded first, cancels this one. The session's loop also wakes at the earliest deadline, checks again, and writes the rotation through the same batch, commit, person-key put and wake as an add or a removal: `rotate::rotate` with no member left out, which chains every head and writes envelopes for the current members only. Two rotations that cross are concurrent again, and step 5 repeats (step 6). The delay is a field of the sync state that the crate's tests set per database.
- Instead of: a fixed delay, which makes two admins online at once rotate together every time; reporting the exposure and leaving the rotation to the app, which RFC 0008 did not take.
- What this does not do: a database without a relay, or on wasm32, never writes it; an admin that holds no key of a head or of a live person key cannot rotate and draws a new deadline at the next pass; the exposure lasts until an admin is online.
- Topics: sdk-rust, sync, membership, rfc-0008
