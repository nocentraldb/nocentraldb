# 2026-11-28 · NOC-599 · A pairing request lives 10 minutes, and a mailbox holds at most eight open, one per member

- Decided by: Carsten, 2026-11-28, on NOC-599 (option A)
- Why: ten minutes leave a person time to take out the device they already hold and confirm, and keep what is open at the relay little; the answer is kept until the ten minutes are over, so a new device that reconnects still gets it. Eight per mailbox leave room for pairing more than one device at once and bound a flood; one per member keeps a poster from filling a mailbox alone. NOC-607's waits count toward the same limits.
- Instead of: 2 minutes with four per mailbox, short for a person who has to find their other device (B); or 1 hour with sixteen, which keeps sealed requests and answers at the relay six times longer (C).
- Topics: pairing, relay, limits, rfc-0019, rfc-0007
