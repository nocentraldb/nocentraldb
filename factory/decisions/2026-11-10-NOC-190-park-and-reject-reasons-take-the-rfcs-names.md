# 2026-11-10 · NOC-190 · The public park and reject reasons take the RFCs' names, and core's finer reasons map onto them

- Decided by: the owner on NOC-193 (the names follow the RFCs), pm on NOC-190 (the mapping, and leaving `UnknownType` out); `UnknownKey` waits for the owner on NOC-199
- Why: `RejectReason` is RFC 0002's step 3 to 5 reasons, `UnknownAuthor`, `BadSignature`, `NotMember`, `NoKey` and `Undecryptable`, one for one with the core. `ParkReason` takes RFC 0002's change reasons, `UnknownSuite`, `UnknownKind`, `UnknownFormat` (steps 2 and 6) and `UnknownPayloadFormat` (step 5a), RFC 0005's `UnknownCollection` and `UnknownField`, and RFC 0004's `UnknownOperation`, `UnknownArgument` and `MalformedArguments`. The core's `UnknownHeaderFormat`, `UnknownHeaderKey` and `UnknownPayloadKey` map to `UnknownFormat`, since RFC 0002 names an unknown header format or "must understand" key in step 2, and an unknown "must understand" payload key in step 6, "unknown format". The core's `UnknownPayloadFormat` covers step 5a and step 6's unknown payload format, which it does not tell apart, and maps to `UnknownPayloadFormat`. The core's operation reason `UnknownKey` has no RFC name; it is built as `UnknownKey`, option A of NOC-199, until the owner answers. A reason the core adds later maps to none until the SDK names it, and a test lists every reason the core has today.
- Instead of: the core's twelve finer reasons as public names, which the owner's answer rules out; or RFC 0005's `UnknownType`, which no core reason produces, as a variant that never occurs.
- What this does not do: `AnotherModel` waits for payload key 64 (its own ticket).
