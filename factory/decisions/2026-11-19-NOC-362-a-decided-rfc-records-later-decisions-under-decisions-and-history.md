# 2026-11-19 · NOC-362 · A decided RFC records later decisions under "Decisions" and in dated History lines, and the gate checks the form

- Decided by: pm, NOC-362, on the coding agent's proposal; it carries out the owner's decision of 2026-11-10 (2026-11-10-NOC-100-rfcs-are-rewritten-as-complete-texts-without-amendments.md: "a decided change is written into the RFC's text with a dated History line, and the decision is kept under 'Decisions'")
- Why: the RFC tests drifted from that decision. They allowed only the publication line in History and no "Decisions" section, so the PRs that changed decided RFCs after 2026-11-10 left no trace in them. Now one helper, `scripts/lib/rfc-record.mjs`, checks every RFC's record in place of those checks. History is the publication line and then one line per later change, "- YYYY-MM-DD · what changed (NOC-n, #m)", in date order, the pull request only where it is known. "Decisions" appears only once History has a later line, stands directly before History, and each entry has a bold dated title, names who decided it and when ("Carsten, YYYY-MM-DD" or "pm, YYYY-MM-DD"), and links its decision file, which must exist, or names its ticket where the decision lives only on the ticket. Each entry is short and leaves the reasons and the options not taken to its decision file. The bans on decision ids, "Rejected options" and "Open questions" stay. The backfill reaches back to 2026-11-10 from the decision log and git, and gives no PR number git does not show.
- Instead of: a Decisions entry only with a decision file, which would leave out the owner's decisions recorded only on their tickets; the reasons and options not taken repeated in every entry, which the decision file already holds; keeping the gate as it was and listing later decisions in a separate page, which a reader of the RFC would not find.
