# 2026-11-22 · NOC-447 · A move erases nothing; an erasure finds the old and the new space by person id

- Decided by: Carsten, 2026-11-22, on RFC 0016's questions, through pm (NOC-449)
- Why: the person key belongs to the person, not the object, and a shred needs a second account's sign-off, which a move does not have.
- Instead of: shredding the old space's person key at the move.
- Topics: move, erasure, rfc-0016
