# 2026-11-11 · NOC-206 · The space status names only the writers who signed a record that still fails

- Decided by: coding agent (NOC-206, after the owner's NOC-222 point 2 A)
- Why: the owner named the field, `SpaceStatus::bad_person_key_records: Vec<Fingerprint>`, the accounts whose records failed their checks (NOC-222). Which failures count was left to the implementation. A fetched record names its writer's account when the log announces the record's generation and hash, the key was not shredded, the writer is a current or former member, the writer's signature verifies, and the member holds the generation's key, but the record does not unwrap or its key does not match the commitment. Then the writer is the one who vouched for the bad bytes. The list is rebuilt from each fetch, in account-key order, kept in memory with the person keys, and kept when a refused batch rebuilds the space's checks.
- Instead of: naming the writer a record claims when its signature does not verify, which would let anyone put another account's name on a bad record; naming the writer of a record the log does not announce yet, or under a generation whose key the member lacks, which may still hold once the member has pulled or received its envelope; accumulating names across fetches, which would keep naming a writer after a correct record replaced the bad one.
- What this does not do: the TypeScript form of the field, which comes with the TypeScript status (NOC-176). The SDK does not write a correct record again on its own when it finds a bad one; a rotation re-wraps every held key (part 3).
