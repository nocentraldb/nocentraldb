# 2026-11-09 · NOC-63 · The open points of nc.ref go to the owner as decisions in two amendments, and RFC 0006 and RFC 0009 follow after

- Decided by: coding agent (NOC-63), for pm's review
- Why: NOC-29 decided what a reference is; the type id, the value's encoding, a value that does not fit, a target in an unlisted collection, a reference into its own space and when a target is not accessible are wire format and merge questions, so RFC 0004 Amendment 4 asks them as decisions 15 to 20, continuing Amendment 1's numbering. The schema form, the index, how a read and a view show the states, subscriptions and modifiers are schema and API questions, so RFC 0005 Amendment 5 asks them as D18 to D22. The examples E15 to E17 follow the recommended options and change if the owner chooses another. RFC 0006 (the index column) and RFC 0009 (read, write, inspect) are left to amendments of their own after the owner decides, so they do not record names the owner has not chosen.
- Instead of: one new RFC for the reference, which the ticket ruled out, or amending RFC 0006 and RFC 0009 now with open names in them.
- Topics: rfc-0004, rfc-0005, references, schema
