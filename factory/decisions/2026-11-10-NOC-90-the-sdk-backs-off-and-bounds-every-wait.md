# 2026-11-10 · NOC-90 · The Rust SDK backs off until a session makes progress, and bounds every wait on the relay

- Decided by: coding agent (NOC-90), on pm's review of #120
- Why: RFC 0007 asks members to back off with jitter ("Liveness") and to retry `rate_limited` after the given time ("The errors"), but says neither what counts as a good session nor how long to wait for an answer. The SDK reconnects after 100 ms, doubling up to 2 minutes, each wait drawn between half and all of it. The backoff starts over only after a session pulled, pushed and subscribed every space once; a session that ends earlier, such as one whose pushes all answer `unavailable`, keeps doubling it. A `rate_limited` error's retry-after is the shortest wait. A local write does not cut a wait short. A pull page that does not move the cursor ends the session, so a relay that serves a range wrongly is retried with backoff instead of asked again at once. The WebSocket handshake and every answer, `pong` included, must arrive within 30 seconds, or the connection counts as lost, and a ping goes out after 5 idle minutes as RFC 0007 says. Messages from the relay are capped at RFC 0007's 128 KiB.
- Instead of: starting the backoff over after every authenticated session, which lets 1,000 members reconnect ten times a second during a backend outage; or waiting without a bound, which keeps a half-open connection `connected` for minutes.
- What this does not do: a `rejected` result `rate_limited` inside `pushed` carries no retry-after, so it waits the backoff alone.
- Topics: sdk-rust, relay, rfc-0007, liveness
