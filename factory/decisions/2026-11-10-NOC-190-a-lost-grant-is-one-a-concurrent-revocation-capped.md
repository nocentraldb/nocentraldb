# 2026-11-10 · NOC-190 · A lost grant is one a concurrent revocation capped, and the SDK filters core's verdict to it

- Decided by: pm (NOC-190, option A), following RFC 0008's text
- Why: RFC 0008, "Concurrent add and remove of one member", defines the lost grant: "Bob's entry is a grant that did not see the removal, so Alice's removal caps it at 'none'. … Bob's entry stays accepted but without effect, and the SDK shows it as a lost grant", and Bob "can repeat the grant once he has seen the removal". The core's `Verdict::Lost` marks every grant whose effective role a revocation lowered below the role it gives (`membership/resolve.rs`), which takes in a grant that a later revocation revoked after seeing it, such as the original add of every member ever removed. So `SpaceStatus::lost_grants` keeps a `Lost` grant only when an accepted revocation of its subject, to a role below the one the grant gives, is concurrent with it: the grant's causal past (`Resolved::seen`) does not hold the revocation, and the revocation's control dependencies, walked through the log, do not reach the grant. The SDK reads the resolved membership and the log, and changes neither; `membership/` stays untouched.
- Instead of: passing core's `Lost` through, which would show a lost grant for every member that was ever removed or demoted; or changing the verdict in `membership/`, outside this ticket's lane (pm files it as a follow-up, after which this filter can go).
- What this does not do: the walk through control dependencies costs one decode per control change in the revocation's causal past, for each `Lost` grant, on each read of the status.
