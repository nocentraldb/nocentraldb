# 2026-11-11 · owner · The daily note leads with the diff on main, what works for a developer, communication, then the owner's decisions and approvals

- Decided by: owner (Carsten, 2026-11-11, in conversation with pm)
- Why: the owner reads the note to steer the project. He wants to see first how main moved, what a developer can build today, and where the texts for developers and contributors stand. Then what he must decide and approve, most important first, and only then the details: merges, tickets done and tickets open.
- Order: (1) the git diff on main between the two notes; (2) what works for a developer now, each point proven by an example application (a, b, c …); (3) communication for (a) developers and (b) contributors: landing page, documentation, RFCs, posts elsewhere; (4) decisions waiting for the owner: needs-owner tickets, most important first; (5) approvals waiting for the owner: PRs, most important first. Then: merged PRs (without the owner, and with his approval), tickets done, tickets open, pm's decisions, RFC feedback.
- Instead of: the earlier note (shipped, merged without the owner, waiting, blocked, pm decided, open questions).
- What this does not do: it does not change what pm may merge or the time of the note. While there is no example application, section 2 says so and names the tests that cover each capability instead.
